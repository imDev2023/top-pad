What happens at the open

  1. The window closes at 14:00:00. Until then you can withdraw everything.
  2. One transaction creates the coin, its pool and the pot's buy. The pot buys first and pays the normal 1.25% fee. Anyone else in that second pays the 99% sniper tax.
  3. Everyone in the pot pays the same price. If more is deposited than the pot can buy, everyone gets an equal share: if you asked for less than the share, you get all of yours. The rest is refunded.
  4. Coins and refunds arrive in your wallet within seconds. Sent by the labelled Greenflag keeper. Nothing to claim, no vesting.
  5. A minute later, you get your Tax Share. Fees snipers pay in the first minute go to the pot's depositors, in proportion to what each deposit bought.

If the coin has not been created by 14:15 UTC, you can withdraw your deposit on chain yourself, without our server.

What Meteora can still do

Meteora's operators can pause a graduated pool and change its fees. Pausing stops trading but never unlocks a position. Meteora can also upgrade its own programs.

What we can still do

Upgrade the Greenflag program, but every upgrade is public 7 days before it lands, so creators can claim their earnings first. The upgrade authority is published and every build is verified.

What only covers our launches

Anyone can create a coin directly on Meteora with our settings. The Creator lock and Vamp Shield only cover coins our launch key co-signed, and only those are listed here.

Trades routed through Jupiter or Titan reach the pool directly. Everything above still applies to them, because it lives on chain, not on this website.
